Rohan Fitness Tracking

A personal fitness-data app. It reads the owner's own activity, heart rate, sleep, workout, and weight data through the Google Health API and produces private daily and weekly fitness summaries.

This app has a single user — the owner. It is not offered to the public, and no data is shared with anyone else.
