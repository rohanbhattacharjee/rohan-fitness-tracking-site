Privacy Policy — Rohan Fitness Tracking

Effective date: September 27, 2026

What this app is

Rohan Fitness Tracking is a personal, single-user app. It is not offered to the public. Only the owner's Google account can authorize it.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's consent, the app reads the following data about the owner, through the Google Health API:

  • Activity and fitness data (steps, distance, active minutes, heart rate, workouts)
  • Sleep data (sleep sessions and stages)
  • Health metrics and measurements (weight, body fat percentage)
  • Basic profile information

How the data is used

The data is used solely to track the owner's personal fitness trends and to generate private daily and weekly summaries for the owner. It is not used for advertising, analytics about other people, or any other purpose.

Data storage and security

Access tokens and fitness data are stored in the owner's private workspace, in files readable only by the owner's own systems. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect the data against unauthorized access.

Data sharing

The app does not share, sell, rent, or transmit the owner's data to any third party, and does not disclose it to any other person.

Data retention and deletion

Data is kept until the owner revokes the app's access or deletes it. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address.
